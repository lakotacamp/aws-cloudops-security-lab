## Notes on Colony Simulator Game Design
I don't play many survival games, but my brothers do and from sampling their opinions, they point to games like minecraft and subnautica for games to reference when designing a survival game. I am coming from a strategy game perspective, games like Civ and the total war games, that play around with concepts of governance, culture, economics, diplomacy and politics. Ultimately the goal is, from the game side, is to create an environment that facilitates culture development from a seed colony settlement simulated through the use of llm models to tell fun and dynamic stories. The game is less about creating a survival game that the user is playing; the game is trying to keep an ai that is playing the survival game alive to see how it uniquely develops and what stories and narratives emerge.
